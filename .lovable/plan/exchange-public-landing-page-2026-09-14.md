# EXCHANGE public landing page

## What will be built
- Replace the student workspace home with a public recruitment landing page.
- Lead with “Your Skills Are Your Currency,” two clear actions, and authentic cross-faculty student collaboration.
- Explain the student problem, the Discover–Exchange–Prove journey, the internal credit loop, and the Skill Passport.
- Close with a community invitation spanning students, faculties, and companies.

## Visual direction
- Deep navy foundation with electric blue and orange accents.
- Modern editorial startup composition with confident typography, sharp section changes, and restrained motion.
- Use the generated Indonesian student collaboration photograph as the primary visual.
- Keep the experience professional and trust-led, avoiding course-marketplace patterns.

## Technical details
- Keep this page frontend-only and use the existing TanStack route and shared button system.
- Update semantic design tokens and page-specific metadata.
- Add working in-page navigation and calls to action.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, interaction targets, overflow, and reduced-motion behavior.
